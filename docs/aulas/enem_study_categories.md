# **ENEM Study Categories**

## **Mathematics and Its Technologies:**

**Basic Mathematics:**
* Percentages.
* Ratios and proportions.
* Rule of three.
* Fractions.
* Divisibility and factoring.
* Powers and roots.
* First- and second-degree equations.
* Inequalities.
* Systems of linear equations.

**Geometry:**
* Plane geometry (area and perimeter).
* Thales' theorem.
* Triangle similarity.
* Pythagorean theorem.
* Metric relations in the right triangle.
* Basic trigonometry.
* Trigonometric cycle and periodic trigonometric functions.
* Circles (arcs, inscribed angles, chords).
* Solid geometry (volume).

**Analytic Geometry:**
* Cartesian plane and points.
* Line equations and slope.
* Circle equations.
* Parallelism, perpendicularity, and relative positions between lines and circles.

**Financial Mathematics:**
* Simple interest.
* Compound interest.
* Inflation.
* Amortization systems.

**Functions:**
* Linear functions.
* Arithmetic progressions (PA).
* Quadratic functions.
* Polynomial and rational functions.
* Exponential functions.
* Geometric progressions (PG).
* Logarithmic functions.

**Statistics and Probability:**
* Mean, median, and mode.
* Standard deviation.
* Analysis of graphs and tables.
* Simple probability.
* Basic combinatorics.

## **Natural Sciences and Their Technologies:**

**Physics:**
* Foundational tools (vectors, SI units, scientific notation, order of magnitude).
* Kinematics.
* Newton's laws.
* Statics and torque (rigid body equilibrium).
* Work and energy.
* Gravitational physics (Universal Gravitation, Kepler's Laws).
* Impulse and momentum.
* Hydrostatics.
* Thermology.
* Wave physics (mechanical waves, acoustics).
* Optics.
* Electrostatics.
* Electrodynamics.
* Electric circuits.
* Magnetism and electromagnetism.

**Chemistry:**
* Atomic structure (atomic models, isotopes, periodic table).
* Chemical bonds and intermolecular forces.
* Materials: properties, physical states, changes of state, metals and alloys.
* Mixtures and separation methods.
* Gases (gas laws, ideal gas equation, kinetic theory, gas mixtures).
* Inorganic functions (acids, bases, salts, oxides).
* Stoichiometry (including mass laws and determination of formulas).
* Solutions (including colloids, suspensions, and colligative properties).
* Chemical kinetics.
* Chemical equilibrium (including pH/pOH, salt hydrolysis, and solubility).
* Thermochemistry.
* Electrochemistry.
* Radioactivity and nuclear reactions (fission, fusion, decay, radioisotopes).
* Organic chemistry (including fermentation).
* Polymers.
* Energy sources and fuels (petroleum, natural gas, coal, biomass, biofuels, nuclear energy, atomic waste).
* Chemical industry, metallurgy, and water treatment.
* Environmental chemistry.

**Biology:**
* Biology as a science (history, scientific method, experimentation).
* Origin of the universe, the Earth, and life.
* Cytology (including origin and evolution of cells).
* Histology.
* Biochemistry.
* Microbiology and virology.
* Systematics, phylogeny, and life cycles.
* Embryology.
* Genetics (including pre-Mendelian ideas, mutations, cancer, genetic counseling).
* Immunology and blood groups (antigens, antibodies, transplants, autoimmune diseases).
* Vaccination.
* Evolution (including artificial selection).
* Ecology.
* Biogeography and Brazilian biomes.
* Conservation of ecosystems and environmental legislation.
* Human physiology.
* Botany.
* Zoology.
* Biotechnology (stem cells, cloning, recombinant DNA, ethical aspects).
* Public health (main diseases in Brazil, STIs, drug use, first aid).
* Social indicators (HDI, poverty and human development).
